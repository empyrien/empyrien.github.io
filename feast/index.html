Each Bahá’í month carries a name — Splendour, Glory, Beauty, Words — and each begins with the Nineteen Day Feast. These programmes gather passages from the Writings on the theme of the month, with links back to the source in every case.

Words · Seventh month · 13 July

Nine readings on the power of the Word — from its origin in the mouth of God to the way we speak to one another. Includes the Persian original of the Lawḥ-i-Maqṣúd with the English beneath.

How these work

Read aloud from anywhere. Every programme has a slideshow mode that fills a screen with one reading at a time, sized to fit — good for a television, a projector, or a phone held at arm’s length.

Share out the readings. Add everyone’s names and assign the passages, in order or shuffled. The slideshow then names whoever is reading next.

Unfamiliar words. Names and older English carry a faint dotted underline; tap one and a plain-English pronunciation appears in the margin, out of the way of the text.

Made to be comfortable. Light and dark, seven text sizes, a high-contrast mode, and a typeface designed for low vision. There is also an optional ambient tone, off unless you ask for it.
